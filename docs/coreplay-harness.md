# CorePlay Lab Harness Principles

User-provided documents are the primary grounding source. Embedded game-design heuristics may
supplement gaps, but they must not silently override document principles.

The system should favor deterministic, reproducible, evidence-backed runs. Major decisions need a
knowledge trace, and conflicts should be resolved explicitly by the Judge Agent.

Keep the MVP narrow: one playable prototype, three levels, one core mechanic, one lightweight
progression element, bounded improvement of the top three weaknesses only, and no external APIs.
